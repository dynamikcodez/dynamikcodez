About

Systems thinker. Reluctant specialist.

I'm a CS student and fullstack developer based in Lagos, formerly an XR developer at IMISI3D — Africa's leading immersive technology lab. I build things that sit at the intersection of technology, culture, and commerce.

My work spans augmented reality, community-owned financial tools, commerce infrastructure for Nigerian SMBs, and long-range research into teleoperation and remote sensing. The thread running through all of it: building for people the tools weren't designed for.

I'm also co-directing PUMZI, a wordless sci-fi graphic novel, and General Secretary of Enactus Babcock University.

How I build

  • Web-First Mandate No app installs. No ARCore or ARKit dependencies. Immersive technology should adapt to the user's infrastructure, not the other way around.
  • Friction Reduction Over Novelty Many problems aren't waiting for new technology. They're waiting for existing tools to be connected more intelligently.
  • Empowering the Underserved The people who most need better tools are usually the last ones those tools are designed for.
  • Infrastructure-First Creativity Creative expression is only as powerful as the systems backing it.
  • Five-Layer Filter Tool → User → Incentive → Distribution → Cultural Context. Will this deploy in Nigeria? Is it economically realistic?

Projects

What I'm building.

Across five themes — each project runs through the same filter. Real problem, real context, real constraints. Some are shipping. Some are resting. All of them are serious.

Theme 1 — Spatial Computing & Cultural Preservation

Cultural Headwear AR

Prototype / Concept

WebAR try-on and cultural context for traditional headwear — face tracking in the browser, no app required. Built for fashion houses, heritage institutions, and tourism boards.

CakeView

Concept / Prototype

AR cake designer for bespoke bakers. Clients visualize and customize orders in 3D before a single thing is baked. Cuts revision cycles and WhatsApp back-and-forth.

Nigeria Tourism AR

Concept

Turns static print media into animated spatial windows. Point a mobile browser at a poster — waterfalls flow, monuments emerge, booking links appear.

AR Food Menus

Active Prototype

Two implementations of 3D food visualization for restaurants — conversational and immersive. Spatial previews reduce ordering friction and increase average order value.

AR Book Platform

Prototype

Layers interactive 3D diagrams and animated processes onto physical textbook pages. No hardware upgrades needed — just a phone and a marker.

Theme 2 — Commerce, Logistics & Community Finance

StoreSmith

Active Development

Commerce OS for Nigerian SMBs. Polished storefronts, payment integration, and vendor infrastructure — for merchants who aren't yet thinking like businesses.

Thanox

Concept — Active Revival

A borderless open-source design collective with one long-term goal: crowdsource the design of an affordable EV for the African market, then bring it to production. The NFT is not the product. The car is.

FamilyFloat

Research & Design

Collaborative financial tool for families and tight-knit social circles. Crypto-native backend on Celo, web2-style interface. Designed for ajo, esusu, and group savings culture.

Community Purchase Club

Concept

Digitizes high-trust offline purchasing collectives. Custom group ledgers, automated contribution tracking, and shared purchasing power.

Theme 3 — Identity, Community & Digital Spaces

Bubble Share

Product Architecture

Permission-based identity platform. Curate distinct profile bubbles for different life contexts — professional, creative, social — and share them selectively via QR.

Social Curation Platform

Concept

For creating and sharing curated collections of newsletters, accounts, playlists, and learning tracks. Human curation packaged into a shareable format.

Theme 4 — Creative Pipelines, Narrative & Cultural Expression

Interactive Narrative Engine

Research & Architecture

Browser-based storytelling platform that lets audiences experience one narrative from multiple character perspectives simultaneously.

AI Creative Pipeline

Research & Workflow Design

Coordinated multi-model framework for complex creative briefs. Built from experiments combining Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini into cohesive multi-modal outputs.

Dance On

MVP Built, Dormant

Interactive dance learning platform using computer vision pose estimation. MVP built, codebase lost. The architecture is solid — this one gets rebuilt.

Motion & Emotion Library

Technical Research

High-fidelity, culturally grounded character animation library for African game and interactive narrative creators. Most existing libraries default to Western behavioral patterns.

Theme 5 — The Outer Horizon

Teleoperation & Remote Marine Exploration

Long-Range Research

XR as the human interface layer for teleoperation — projecting skilled presence anywhere in the world without emigrating. Deep focus: connecting classrooms in Lagos to the ocean floor via affordable ROVs and WebXR.
